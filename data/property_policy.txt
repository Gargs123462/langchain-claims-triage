PROPERTY POLICY - COVERAGE SUMMARY

Policy Type: Homeowners Property Insurance
Coverage A - Dwelling: Covers the physical structure of the home against fire, wind, hail, and other listed perils, up to the policy limit of $350,000.

Coverage B - Personal Property: Covers personal belongings inside the home (furniture, electronics, clothing) up to 50% of the Dwelling limit. Deductible: $1,000 per claim.

Coverage C - Loss of Use: If the home becomes uninhabitable due to a covered loss, this pays for temporary living expenses (hotel, meals) up to $25,000 or 12 months, whichever comes first.

Water Damage: Sudden and accidental water damage (burst pipe, appliance failure) is covered. Gradual leaks, flooding, and sewer backup are NOT covered under this policy and require separate riders.

Claim Filing Window: Claims must be reported within 60 days of discovering the damage.

Exclusions: This policy does not cover damage from earthquakes, floods, neglect, or intentional acts by the insured. Business-related property is not covered under this residential policy.
