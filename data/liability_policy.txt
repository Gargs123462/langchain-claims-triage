LIABILITY POLICY - COVERAGE SUMMARY

Policy Type: General Liability Insurance
Coverage A - Bodily Injury Liability: Covers costs if a third party is injured on the insured's property or due to the insured's actions, up to $500,000 per occurrence.

Coverage B - Property Damage Liability: Covers damage the insured causes to someone else's property, up to $250,000 per occurrence.

Coverage C - Legal Defense Costs: Covers attorney fees and legal costs if the insured is sued over a covered incident, in addition to (not subtracted from) the coverage limits above.

Medical Payments: Covers immediate medical expenses for a third party injured on the insured's property, up to $10,000, regardless of fault, without requiring a lawsuit.

Claim Filing Window: Claims must be reported within 45 days of the incident. Any lawsuit or legal notice must be forwarded to the insurer within 5 business days of receipt.

Exclusions: This policy does not cover intentional harm, contractual liability assumed by the insured, or injuries to the insured's own employees (covered separately under Workers' Compensation).
